## Authorized Startle production — 2026-11-22

This section supersedes the initial archive-only status above. The user approved 15 revised ambush concepts and their artwork/CSV installation, then approved the third Startle frame preview. The user separately authorized Startle bounds in the active set Draw.lua and STARTLENAME in Examples/Run Bun!/Styles.ini.

Startle means a sneaky Dog hiding in cover, waiting to pounce, then abruptly emerging. Show the hiding place and sudden reveal clearly. Most Buns freeze, recoil, jump, drop things or stumble in startled panic; at most one or two begin running. Do not substitute a general Chase scene. Maintain reference Bun bodies, faces and paws and varied enormous demonic Dogs.

Approved frame: Images/Components/Frame Startle.png, 825 x 1125. Artwork bounds at base size: X 56, Y 202, Width 714, Height 468. Artwork PNG: 1428 x 936, scaled proportionally. CSV Type: Startle. Each installed image gets its matching CSV row immediately, blank Description/Flavor/Sex. User approved omitting question marks from card names because Windows filenames cannot contain them.

### Approved revised roster

1. EVERYBUN RUN!
Warm sunlit elaborate Victorian market. A huge HORNLESS dark brindle mastiff with gold eyes was crouched inside an unoccupied deep canvas-backed produce stall. It suddenly bursts through striped canvas backing, rear still inside the shadowed stall, front paws airborne OVER the splintering low counter toward five unaware shoppers. Torn canvas visibly clings to shoulders and frames hiding place. Foreground cream female in teal dress jerks back dropping a carrot basket, rusty male vendor freezes with hands up, grey female jumps in place and two adult customers recoil. Only one distant Bun turns to run. Buns retain trim bodies, not all kids. Colorful market buildings and copper street lamps. Distinct poses and faces, no assistance or betrayal.

2. Wrong End of the Alley
Rainy blue-hour narrow cobbled alley. A massive ash-grey wolfish Dog with short swept-back horns, piercing ice-blue eyes and brass collar lunges sideways from deep concealment behind tall stacked delivery crates under a loading canopy. Rear disappears behind crates, forelegs reach into passage just in front of three unsuspecting Buns. Front spotted female in burgundy long coat slams heels down and leans backward, white male drops rolled plans, charcoal female recoils hands to face. Keep most stationary, no one running in parallel. Crates large enough to conceal Dog, coherent pavement, warmly lit windows.

3. Who Rang That!
Inside an old Victorian steampunk general store, low viewpoint along counter. A small tan female Bun in plum coat has just pressed a brass service bell on counter. A giant hornless sable Dog concealed in shadowed stockroom behind counter suddenly thrusts upper body and jaws OVER counter through hanging split drapes, back still hidden. Bell is ordinary shop bell, not an alarm scene. Customer leaps backward with four-digit palms raised and dropped shopping bag; grey male companion freezes open-mouthed clutching hat. Counter height and back room depth accommodate large Dog; brass scales, colorful jars, intricate till, shelves. Clear diagonal threatening lunge, no contact/injury.

4. It Can Climb!
Late-afternoon rooftop terrace with little brass telescope and tea table. Giant russet-black short-coated demonic Dog with amber eyes and rugged hornless brows springs UP over a high parapet from unseen exterior ledge, powerful front paws hooking coping while hindquarters remain below wall, jaw snapping forward. Three adult Buns enjoying terrace recoil in startled terror: grey female in green dress drops teacup, cream male in navy waistcoat flings hands up, brown female stumbles backward; only fourth begins scrambling toward chimney. Coherent rooftop ledge supporting climb, not floating Dog. Beautiful steampunk city skyline.

5. Not a Tunnel!
Low camera at floor of vast idle steampunk mill. A dark apparent passage beneath a massive raised machine actually conceals a giant black-tan mastiff Dog. It snaps its head and one forepaw OUT of deep machinery shadow, jaws suddenly surrounding the dark gap; visible body recess behind makes ambush clear. Two small trim adult Buns with toolbox and lantern recoil backward in surprise just before approaching, one drops lantern onto intact cobbles, one raises palms; neither already running. Show adequate space under machine for Dog, supported heavy beams and coherent stationary gears. Green jacket spotted male and mauve-coated cream female. Warm lamplight and cool iron.

6. Too Many Feet!
Sunny lavish garden party. Enormous white-and-charcoal piebald hornless demonic Dog with green eyes suddenly pounces THROUGH a dense tall hedge, branches parting around torso and rear hidden in foliage. Five finely dressed adult Buns at tea startle into awkward collisions near a SMALL open garden gate: dropped saucer, lifted hands, one falling back onto bottom, others jerking backward, at most one turning through gate. No helping or intentional shoving. Enough garden space and hedge depth to hide Dog. Flowers, brass conservatory, lace tablecloth, steampunk hats, several large bare rounded rabbit paws.

7. The Floor Is Moving!
Inside an elevated timber riverside freight depot. Giant muscular dark brown demonic Dog with low curved horns was concealed in shadowed crawlspace UNDER raised loading platform. It suddenly thrusts head, shoulders and forepaws upward through a loose section of plank flooring; boards lift around the opening, rear stays below. Three adult worker Buns on INTACT surrounding boards recoil and jump from shock, parcels tumbling, no one standing unsupported over hole. Short legs and broad rounded boots, cute varied caps and goggles. Clear platform joists and visible edge establish crawlspace big enough for Dog; interesting winch and steampunk freight gear.

8. Don't Look Behind You
Victorian railway waiting room. Giant tawny-black hornless demonic hound was hidden in a deep shadowed service alcove behind heavy teal curtains, suddenly lunges OUT toward foreground grey male Bun in plum travel coat holding suitcase. Curtains part and drag behind Dog shoulders. Main Bun just starts to twist around in shock; two companions facing him see Dog first, one freezes with both hands up and one drops tickets, eyes huge, no one running yet. Wide alcove large enough for Dog, rich tile floor, bronze clock, leather benches. View three-quarter side, Dog not already biting.

9. That Wasn't Thunder
Rainy twilight beside a grand carriage house. Huge black-and-rust muscular demonic Dog with subtle short horns and violet eyes pounces from DEEP dark open carriage bay, rear in bay and front emerging into awning shelter. Four Buns who had been calmly waiting out storm recoil simultaneously, umbrellas tilt, one drops parcel, one jumps straight back, only one begins fleeing. Steampunk carriage visible in bay beyond enough room for hidden predator, wet cobbles and lightning beyond. Cute adult proportions, varied full clothing, purposeful brass lanterns and drainage.

10. Somebody Sneezed
Warm dusty Victorian warehouse, sharp detailed sunbeams without blur. Little cream female Bun in blue coat has just sneezed into handkerchief, caught startled with mouth open and ears up. Huge grey-brown hornless demonic Dog with yellow eyes suddenly springs from concealment behind high flour sacks, sack stack splitting at edge, rear still behind stack. Two male worker Buns in distinct aprons recoil dropping a scoop and ledger. NOT Buns hiding from a passing Dog; Dog was waiting unseen. Clearly visible gap in sack stack, strong ground contact and shadow, steampunk weighing scales and lifts.

11. Occupied!
Autumn freight siding. A Bun porter has just SLID a heavy rail freight-car door sideways along its horizontal exterior rail; door now clearly stacked against car side leaving opening. Giant cream-coated demonic Dog with black muzzle and red eyes launches forequarters OUT of dark freight car, rear still inside, snapping jaws and forepaws reaching down. Two porters below recoil in shock, one drops luggage, the other backsteps hands up. One wealthy female passenger freezes nearby. Train stationary, believable car clearance, door mechanism and tracks coherent, no wheels through characters. Colorful steampunk station and falling autumn leaves, all sharp.

12. A Nose for Trouble
Overcast walled garden beside a tall thick flowering hedge. Massive brindled Dog with a pale scarred muzzle and cyan eyes bursts through hedge at the instant two inquisitive Buns had noticed its nose. Branches crack around forehead and shoulders, rear concealed in thick foliage, one forepaw thrusting toward garden path. Trim cinnamon male in olive waistcoat jumps backward dropping pruning shears, white-black female in rose dress recoils hands raised, ears bolt upright. Show big nose leading monstrous snapping head without cartoon distortion; rich flowering plants and brass irrigation system. Immediate surprise, not running chase.

13. Peekaboo!
Inside huge Victorian conservatory at sunset. A curious adult Bun draws back a long burgundy curtain covering deep maintenance alcove beside glazed wall; a gigantic dark auburn hornless demonic Dog with green eyes suddenly lunges OUT from hidden recess through opening. One curtain remains hanging, pulled curtain gripped by cream male Bun whose body recoils sharply; grey female drops flowerpot and tan male freezes hands raised. All dressed steampunk gardeners, compact trim build. Curtain pocket deep enough for Dog, coherent arch and lush potted plants, copper heating pipes. No helping, no bite contact.

14. Mind the Gap!
Misty dawn ornate timber footbridge over a rocky shallow stream, coherent bridge deck, rails and supports. Giant muscular slate-grey demonic Dog with narrow backward horns and amber eyes hidden on broad rock shelf UNDER bridge suddenly springs upward beside the right railing, forepaws gripping rail as jaws appear at Bun height; rear and launching shelf still visible below edge. Three adult Buns crossing with breakfast parcels recoil in place, one drops bread, one jumps back, one crouches in surprise. NOT Dog jumping through solid deck, no impossible unsupported structure. Beautiful steampunk village, gas lamps, reeds, clear depth, no motion blur.

15. Surprise Delivery
Large Victorian receiving depot. An enormous reinforced shipping crate big enough to conceal a giant Dog suddenly bursts outward as hornless massive black-white patch-coated demonic Dog with orange eyes lunges from within, rear still in dark crate, hinged front panels breaking off clear of characters. Four adult receiving clerks recoil: female in blue jumps backward dropping clipboard, rust male drops stamp, grey male freezes hands up, one small distant clerk begins to run. Crate has open interior space and remaining side/back panels, wood fragments don't impale anyone. Brass freight scales, pneumatic mail tubes, pallets, daylight, vivid material detail. Strong readable surprise and cute trim Buns.
