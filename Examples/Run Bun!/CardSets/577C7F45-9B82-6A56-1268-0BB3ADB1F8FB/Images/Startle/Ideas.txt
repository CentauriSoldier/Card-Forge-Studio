Run Bun! — Startle card concepts
Saved 2026-11-22 for later discussion about dog cards.
These are Startle concepts, not Panic concepts. No artwork has been generated.

1. EVERYBUN RUN!
A crowded Victorian market erupts as a gigantic red-eyed dog bursts through an awning. Buns scatter beneath airborne carrots, hats, and overturned baskets.

2. Wrong End of the Alley
Buns skid around a corner and find a huge dog blocking the narrow passage. Those in front desperately backpedal into the ones still charging forward.

3. Who Rang That?!
A Bun accidentally pulls a factory alarm cord. Huge brass bells shake overhead while distant canine silhouettes turn toward the sound.

4. It Can Climb?!
Buns perched on a rooftop stare down in horror as enormous claws curl over the gutter. One Bun is already scrambling up the chimney.

5. Not a Tunnel!
A fleeing Bun dives toward a dark opening beneath machinery—then notices teeth around its edges. The supposed escape route is a waiting dog's mouth.

6. Too Many Feet!
Several Buns try to squeeze through a tiny fence opening at once. Ears, boots, and coats tangle while a monstrous dog's shadow stretches over them.

7. The Floor Is Moving!
Buns discover that the factory platform beneath them is an active conveyor belt. It carries them toward an open loading door where a dog waits below.

8. Don't Look Behind You
A running Bun sees its companions' terrified expressions before noticing the enormous jaws closing in behind its shoulder. Its little brass rear-view mirror shows the teeth.
