# Run Bun! — Panic artwork

Read ../../../GEN.md and the Bun and Dog GEN.md guides before future work. Current user directions take precedence.

## Approved direction

Panic cards help the Buns survive a Chase. Do not confuse these with the eight Startle concepts archived in ../Startle/Ideas.txt.

Dogs must be MASSIVE EVIL DEMON DOGS, with powerful canine builds and unmistakably demonic faces, jaws, horns/ridges and sinister eyes. Preserve individual designs. Buns follow the approved Katie/Eddie family style and anatomy. Full-color Victorian steampunk, sharp detail throughout, coherent actions and mechanisms, no baked-in text or frames.

Too Cute To Eat is the deliberate expression exception: the enormous demonic Dog looks sadly and pityingly at a lone crying child Bun, without copious Dog tears. The town square must be deserted AND destroyed. The user called the baby Bun in the first generated version PERFECT: preserve that child's design, outfit, pose, proportions and crying expression in future edits. The preserved reference is Resize/Before Demon Correction/Too Cute To Eat.png. The revised installed scene retains a close visual match, not a verified pixel-identical child.

Hidey Hole retains its approved concept: a Bun shelters in a burrow while an enormous Dog tries to reach it. Its old prototype was rebuilt for the current art style and landscape format.
