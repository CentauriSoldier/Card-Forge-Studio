# Run Bun! — Lure artwork generation and editing protocol

Read [shared rules](../../../GEN.md), [Bun anatomy and style](../Bun/GEN.md), and [Dog design](../Dog/GEN.md). No application code changes are authorized by this guide.

## Direction

Lures show temptation before terror: the viewer notices danger before the adorable Bun does. Bait is appealing and colorful; Buns are curious, hopeful, or delighted. Hidden Dogs and traps create dread without turning the scene into another chase or monster portrait. Vary bait, locations, poses, mechanisms, and how the danger is revealed. Preserve full color and sharp focus throughout, including backgrounds. Use installed approved images directly as style references.

Traps MUST be physically believable: connected ropes, supported loads, clear triggers, sensible anchors and pulleys, and enough room to capture the Bun. No floating hardware or ropes passing through objects. Avoid gratuitous machinery. Keep the mechanism readable at card size without adding instructional labels.

For I'm a Genius!, retain the propped-crate/carrot concept and hidden Dog holding the pull cord. The Bun must crouch or duck, NOT crawl or lie on its belly. Use an inverted open-bottom crate with its rear edge resting on the ground, front edge held by a removable prop, carrot inside its footprint, and a cord positioned to pull the prop out.

Buns retain adorable youthful family proportions, unique natural fur/outfits, four digits per hand (three fingers and a thumb), four toes, and rounded fingernails. Dogs are huge, powerful, demonic, slightly steampunk, with exactly two eyes and restrained sinister colors. No candy-colored coats or neon decoration.

## Dimensions and files

Current Draw.lua Lure opening at 825 × 1125 is approximately X 47, Y 194, width 730, height 481. Install landscape PNGs at 1460 × 962, aspect 730:481. Scale proportionally; never stretch independently. Keep bait, faces, and crucial trap details away from frame overlaps.

Use Images/Lure/Name.png and CSV Type Lure. Follow shared backups, revision preservation, immediate CSV registration, and verification rules. Leave Description and Flavor blank. Preserve the original I'm a Genius! before replacement.
