## Glossary

<!-- section: glossary-dog; source: 0 -->
### Dog

The character that pursues the **Buns**, or the player controlling it.


<!-- section: glossary-bun-bunny; source: 0 -->
### Bun / Bunny

Interchangeable terms for a character controlled by a **Bun** player.

<!-- section: glossary-brace; source: 0 -->
### Brace

The pair of **Buns** belonging to one player.

<!-- section: glossary-burrow; source: 0 -->
### Burrow

A section on a **Bun** card. Each escaped **Bun** gives its player up to one play using that section per **Dog** turn.

<!-- section: glossary-chase; source: 0 -->
### Chase

A **Dog** card that starts a pursuit. The pursuit itself is called a **Chase**.

<!-- section: glossary-lure; source: 0 -->
### Lure

A **Dog** card that baits a **Bun** or sets up a **Chase**.

<!-- section: glossary-sniff; source: 0 -->
### Sniff

A **Dog** card that reveals **Buns**. It cannot catch them; catching requires a **Chase**.

<!-- section: glossary-catch-roll-escape-roll; source: 0 -->
### Catch Roll / Escape Roll

The **Dog**’s roll is its **catch roll**. The **Bun**’s opposed roll is its **escape roll**.

<!-- section: glossary-nice-nasty; source: 0 -->
### Nice / Nasty

**Nice** cards help another player's **Bun**. **Nasty** cards make trouble for another player's **Bun**.

<!-- section: glossary-panic; source: 0 -->
### Panic

A **Reaction** card you can play when one of your **Buns** is chased.

<!-- section: glossary-reaction; source: 0 -->
### Reaction

A card played in response to an event. For example, **Panic** responds to a **Chase** targeting one of your **Buns**.

<!-- section: glossary-chase-modifier; source: 0 -->
### Chase Modifier

The value printed on a **Bun** card that adjusts its **escape roll** in a **Chase**.

<!-- section: glossary-redirect; source: 0 -->
### Redirect

An effect that sends a **Chase** toward a different **Bun**.

<!-- section: glossary-natural-roll; source: 0 -->
### Natural Roll

The number showing on the die before modifiers.

<!-- section: glossary-dusk-dawn; source: 0 -->
### Dusk / Dawn

At **Dusk**, score the day. At **Dawn**, reset the **Braces** for the next day.

<!-- section: glossary-last-chase; source: 0 -->
### Last Chase

The roll-off that decides the winner when the **Dog** ties for first.
