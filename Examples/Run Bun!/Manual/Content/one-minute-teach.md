## 🥕 The one-minute teach

**3–8 players · 1 Dog · 2 Buns per Bun player · 3 days**

A fast, loud steampunk **Chase**. Help a rival get away when the **Dog** is ahead.
Send the **Chase** their way when they’re pulling ahead of you.

One player is the **Dog**. Everyone else starts with two **Bun**, called a
**Brace**. **Buns** enter play face down; only their **Brace**’s owner may look at
them. The **Dog** draws cards and plays **Chase** to pursue a **Bun**. Its player
can react with **Panic**, then both sides roll a d20. If the **Dog** wins, it
catches the **Bun** and chooses to eat or injure it. If the **Bun** wins,
it escapes for the day.

Every **Bun** you get to safety keeps you in the action from the **Burrow**.
Injured **Buns** sit out until **Dawn**. Once every **Bun** has escaped, been
eaten, or been injured, **Dusk** falls and everyone scores. At **Dawn**, escaped
and injured **Buns** return to their **Braces**, and fresh **Buns** take the
places of any the **Dog** ate. Get the same **Bun** out again and its escape is
worth even more.

After Day 3, add up the scores. The highest total wins. Whether you played
the table to keep your **Brace** alive or timed your **Chases** to catch the
**Buns** that mattered most, three days of sharp decisions earned you the
win.
