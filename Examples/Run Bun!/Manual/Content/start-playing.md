## Start Playing

1. **Draw characters.** The **Dog** player draws one card from the **Dog** character
   deck and places it face up in front of them. Then, in clockwise order
   from the **Dog**, each **Bun** player draws two **Bun** cards and places them face
   down in front of them. These two **Buns** form that player's **Brace**. Only
   that **Brace**’s owner may look at these face-down cards. Both
   start in play with no previous escapes.
2. **Draw starting hands.** In turn order, each player draws their starting
      hand from their side's draw pile. The **Dog** draws 5 cards or one per **Bun**
      player, whichever is higher. Each **Bun** player draws 5 cards.
3. **Start Day 1.** Begin with the first **Dog** turn. Keep a record of each
   day's scores and each **Bun**'s escapes for later days.
