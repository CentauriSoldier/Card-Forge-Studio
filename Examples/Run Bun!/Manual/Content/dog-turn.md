## Take a **Dog** Turn 🐕

Draw cards, make plays and resolve any **Chases**, then discard down to the
current hand limits. Take another **Dog** turn if any **Buns** are still in
play. Once all **Buns** are out of play for the day, score at **Dusk**.

### Draw cards and check play allowances

The **Dog** draws **one card per Bun player** each turn. At a five-player
table, the **Dog** draws four. The **Dog**’s maximum hand size is **5 or the number
of Bun players, whichever is higher**. Its **Havoc** deck mixes **Chase**,
**Lure**, **Sniff**, and **Startle** cards, which count toward the same **Dog** play
limit. The size of that play limit is still open.

A **Bun** player’s maximum hand size is **3 + 1 for each Bun in play or
escaped**. Each **Bun**’s status also sets how many cards its player draws
and may play. A play requires an eligible card in hand.

| **Bun** status | Draws per **Dog** turn | Maximum plays per **Dog** turn |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| In play | 1 | **Up to 1 regular play** |
| Escaped | 1 | **Up to 1 Burrow play** |
| Injured | 0 | **0 plays** |
| Eaten | 0 | **0 plays** |

With two **Buns** in play, you may make **up to two regular plays**. With one
in play and one escaped, you may make **up to one regular play and up to one
Burrow play**. Once both escape, you may make **up to two Burrow plays**, using
only the **Burrow** section of your cards. If the **Dog** **Chases** one of your **Buns**,
you may play **up to one Panic card** for that **Chase**, separately from these plays.

**Empty deck.** When a deck runs out, shuffle its discard pile to make a new draw pile. Continue any remaining draws.

### Hidden **Buns**

**Buns** enter play face down. Only their **Brace**’s owner may look at them.
Being targeted by a **Chase** does not reveal a **Bun**. Reveal it when it is
caught or when a revealing effect, such as a **Sniff** card, reveals it.

When a **Dog** card affects a hidden **Bun**, that **Bun**’s owner must honestly
report whether the effect applies. **Sniff** cards reveal **Buns** and cannot
catch them; catching requires a **Chase**.

### 🎲 Resolve a **Chase**

1. **Choose the target.** The **Dog** plays a **Chase** card and picks an eligible
   **Bun** in play. A **Bun** can be chased only once per **Dog** turn. How **Redirects**
   count toward that limit is still open.
2. **React.** The targeted **Bun**’s player can play one **Panic** card. Apply
   any legal modifiers before rolling. The **Chase** goes ahead even if the
   **Bun** player declines to react.
3. **Roll.** The **Dog** makes its **catch roll** and the **Bun** makes its
   **escape roll**, each using a d20. Against a face-down **Bun**, the **Dog**
   takes **−6 to its catch roll**. Check **Natural Rolls** first. If neither
   side rolled a **Natural 1** or **Natural 20**, compare the totals after the
   **Bun**’s printed **Chase** modifier, the face-down penalty to the **Dog**’s
   **catch roll** if applicable, and any card modifiers.

| **Natural Rolls** | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Both roll **1**, or both roll **20** | Both reroll. |
| **Dog** rolls **20**, or **Bun** rolls **1** | The **Dog** catches the **Bun**. |
| **Bun** rolls **20**, or **Dog** rolls **1** | The **Bun** escapes. |
| Neither rolls a 1 or 20 | Higher **Dog** total means a catch. Higher **Bun** total means an escape. |

In a regular **Chase**, a **Natural 20** earns **+1 point** and a **Natural 1**
costs **1 point** for the side that rolled it. Apply both adjustments if
both sides qualify. The **Bun** adjustment goes to the targeted **Bun**'s
player. Count only the roll that resolves the **Chase**. Record these points
for **Dusk** in addition to the usual outcome scores.

**Example:** Against a revealed **Bun**, the **Dog**’s **catch roll** is **14**.
The **Bun**’s **escape roll** is **12** with a printed **+3 Chase Modifier**.
The **Bun**’s total is **15**, so it escapes immediately.

**Buns** stay in play until chased. Winning a **Chase** is enough to escape for
the day. There are no Escape cards.

#### 🐕 Eat or Injure

When the **Dog** catches a **Bun**, reveal that **Bun** and choose one.

- **Eat it.** Remove that **Bun** from the game permanently. The **Dog** earns **+1**,
  the **Bun** player loses **1 point**, and the usual **Brace** bonuses apply.
- **Injure it.** The **Bun** is **injured** and sits out for the rest of the
  day. The **Dog** rolls a d20. A roll of **1–10** gives an injury multiplier
  of **1**. A roll of **11–20** gives a multiplier of **2**. Multiply
  **(previous escapes + 1)** by the injury multiplier
  and subtract that many points from the **Bun**’s player. Count only escapes
  by that same **Bun** on earlier days. The **Dog** scores no points for releasing it.

| Earlier escapes | **Bun** player | **Dog** |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0 | **−1 or −2** | 0 |
| 1 | **−2 or −4** | 0 |
| 2 | **−3 or −6** | 0 |

An injured **Bun** cannot be chased again that day and gives its owner no **Burrow** play.

### Finish the **Dog** turn

**End the turn.** After all plays and **Chases** for the full turn are finished,
each player discards down to their current maximum hand size. Use each
**Bun**’s status at the end of the turn to calculate its player’s limit.
