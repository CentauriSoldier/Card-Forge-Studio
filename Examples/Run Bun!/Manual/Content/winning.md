## 🏁 Who wins

Add each player’s scores from all three days. **The highest score wins.**
**Bun** players tied for first share the win. If the **Dog** ties for first with
one or more **Bun** players, settle it with **Last Chase**.

The **Dog** can score from any **Brace**. That gives **Bun** players a reason to
cooperate when the **Dog** is ahead. A veteran **Bun** is also a tempting target
for rivals. On Day 3, a **Bun** with two earlier escapes can score **+4** by
escaping or **−1** if eaten, even before the **Brace** bonus. A **Redirect** can
sabotage its owner or give them the escape they needed.

### 🎲 **Last Chase**

Each tied **Bun** player chooses one **Bun** from their **Brace** for a final opposed
d20 roll against the **Dog**. Use that **Bun**’s printed **Chase** modifier and the usual
**Natural 1**, **Natural 20**, and reroll rules. No cards are played. **Natural Rolls**
settle the **Chase** outcome, and the final scores stay the same.

- **The Dog catches every tied Bun:** the **Dog** wins.
- **One tied Bun escapes:** its player wins.
- **Several tied Buns escape:** their players share the win.
