RUN, BUN! — FULL SUMMARY (concise)

Type: PvP party game

Players: 3–8

Roles:

Exactly 1 Dog

All other players are Bunny players

Bunnies:

Each Bunny player controls 2 Bunnies

Turn order:

Dog always goes first

Dog turn:

Dog gets 1 chase per Bunny player

Each chase targets one Bunny

A Bunny may only be chased once per Dog turn

Bunny play:

Bunnies are reactive only (play cards when targeted)

Bunny-on-bunny betrayal is intentional and encouraged

Win conditions:

Dog wins if it eats both Bunnies of any one Bunny player

Bunnies win if any Bunny player gets both of their Bunnies to escape

When one Bunny escapes, start a 3-Dog-turn timer:

If the partner Bunny does not escape in time, the escaped Bunny returns

When a Bunny player wins by escaping both Bunnies, all remaining Bunnies are eaten immediately

Card types so far:

Bone

Bunny: guaranteed escape (not counterable)

Dog: +1 extra chase this turn

Carrot

Bunny: redirect chase to another Bunny

Dog: lock target (no redirect)

Hidey Hole

Bunny: remove self from play for 1 turn (not last Bunny)

Dog: +2 extra chases this turn

Theme & tone:

Steampunk cartoon

Cute visuals, mean gameplay

Fast, loud, social, betrayal-driven

Design goal:

Explainable in ~1 minute

Minimal rules, high table talk, constant pressure
