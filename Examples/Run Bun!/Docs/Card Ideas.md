# Card Ideas

## Trip Doge

*Panic*

> After the **Dog** rolls against your **Bunny**, but before the **Chase** is
> resolved, make the **Dog** reroll its d20. Your **Bunny** keeps its roll. The
> **Dog** must use the new roll for the **Chase** outcome and any **Natural Roll**
> points.

**Timing:** Play after seeing the dice. This uses your one **Panic** for that
**Chase**.
